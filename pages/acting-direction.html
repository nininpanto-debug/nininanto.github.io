ABOUT ME

Passion Beyond Performance

Acting is more than delivering dialogues. It is about understanding emotions, building believable characters and creating moments that remain with the audience long after the screen fades to black.

Alongside acting, I am passionately learning the craft of direction—exploring storytelling, visual language, character development and cinematic expression.

Every project, every challenge and every performance adds another chapter to my journey. This page is a reflection of my growth as an actor, an aspiring director and a lifelong student of cinema.
