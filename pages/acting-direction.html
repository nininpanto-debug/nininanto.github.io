MY STORY

The Dream That Was Delayed... Never Destroyed.

My passion for acting began long before I stepped onto a professional set. School and college introduced me to the world of mono acting, drama and mime, where I discovered the joy of becoming someone else on stage.

Like many dreamers, life took me in different directions. Responsibilities, work and personal challenges temporarily moved me away from cinema, but they never took cinema out of me.

Every experience I gained outside the film industry made me a stronger performer. Meeting people, leading teams, facing failures and starting again helped me understand real human emotions that I now bring into every character I play.

Today, I continue my journey as an actor while learning the art of filmmaking as an aspiring director. My goal is not only to perform memorable characters but also to create stories that leave a lasting impact on audiences.

"Every audition is an opportunity. Every role is a lesson. Every story brings me one step closer to my dream."
